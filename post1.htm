Pickup & Getting There — Smooth Forest Entry

Pickup from Lonavala Station: The easiest way to begin your Rajmachi journey — cabs or jeeps meet you at the station and drive you straight to the Udhewadi base village.

Route Options:

  • From Lonavala: Streamlined and scenic — great for families and beginner trekkers.
  • From Karjat (via Kondana Caves): More terrain challenge, cave views, and waterfalls — a mini adventure in itself.

Self-Drive Tips:

  • The final stretch (~16 km) to the campsite is tough off-road — go only if your vehicle has enough clearance (SUVs preferred).

Local Charges: Forest or local authorities may levy small fees — usually charged at the base or parking area.

History of Rajmachi Fort

Rajmachi isn’t just a trekking point — it’s a historic landmark. Built during the Satavahana dynasty and later fortified by the Marathas, Rajmachi Fort has seen centuries of battles, rulers, and trade routes. The twin forts — Shrivardhan and Manaranjan — were used to monitor Bor Ghat, a crucial trade route connecting Mumbai and Pune. Standing at the fort, you don’t just see hills and valleys — you feel centuries of stories whispering through the stones.

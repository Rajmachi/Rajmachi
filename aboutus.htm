About Rajmachi Campsite

Where mist meets memories, and nature whispers peace.

Our Story

Nestled deep in the Sahyadri range, Rajmachi Campsite is more than just tents under stars — it’s an escape to soulful serenity. Imagine waking up to chirping birds, misty air wrapping around your soul, and a cup of piping hot chai served under the open sky. From guided jungle treks to the mystical Shree Shrivardhan and Manoranjan forts, every moment here is dipped in adventure and purity.

Stay in Scenic Tents

Comfortable bedding, stargazing nights, and bonfire tales — our tents bring you closer to nature without compromising coziness.

Desi Homemade Meals

Freshly cooked Maharashtrian thalis served with warmth, just like home. Breakfast to dinner — we’ve got you covered.

Guided Treks & Waterfalls

Explore lush forests, ancient forts, and seasonal waterfalls with our expert local guides. Each trail is a hidden story.
